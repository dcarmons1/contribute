## Get Involved

Join thousands of volunteers who help learners around the world every day. Here are some meaningful ways you can contribute:

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Help Others" icon="pen">
    [Support fellow coders](https://forum.freecodecamp.org) by answering their questions in our friendly community forum.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Give Feedback" icon="magnifier">
    [Review and give feedback](https://forum.freecodecamp.org/c/project-feedback?max_posts=1) on real projects created by other learners.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Translate Content" icon="translate">
    [Translate freeCodeCamp](https://www.freecodecamp.org/news/tag/translations/) lessons and articles to make learning accessible to everyone.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Contribute Code" icon="seti:json">
    [Join our open-source projects](/getting-started/#learning-platform) and help improve our learning platform.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
